Privacy Policy: 123 Trace & Count

Last updated: 25 September 2026

123 Trace & Count is a numbers game for young children. This policy covers the Android and iOS app (package com.number_tracing_game).

Summary

The app does not collect, use, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no analytics or tracking.

Information stored on the device

The stars a child earns and the sound and voice settings are saved only on the device, using the device's own app storage. They never leave the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled. Parents can also erase them in the app under For parents → Reset progress.

Network access and third parties

The app does not request internet access and does not include any third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Numbers are spoken using the text-to-speech engine that is built into the device. That engine is part of the device's operating system and is governed by the device maker's privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not request any Android or iOS permissions.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and to meet the Google Play Families Policy. Because no personal information is collected, none is disclosed to third parties. The parents area is protected by a question for grown-ups, and it contains no links out of the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's issue page.
