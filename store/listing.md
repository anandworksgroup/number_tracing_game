# Google Play store listing

Draft copy and asset checklist for the Play Console (*Grow → Store presence → Main store listing*).
Rebuild the graphics with `npm run store-assets` after changing the app.

## App details

**App name** (max 30 characters)

> 123 Trace & Count: Kids Math

**Short description** (max 80 characters)

> Learn to write numbers 0–20 with guided tracing, counting and balloon games.

**Full description** (max 4000 characters)

> Help your child learn to write and count numbers with 123 Trace & Count, a bright and friendly numbers game made for toddlers and preschoolers.
>
> ✏️ TRACE NUMBERS 0 TO 20
> • Follow the green dot and the moving arrows to write each number the right way
> • A helping hand shows every stroke before your child begins
> • Numbered badges show stroke order for numbers like 4, 5 and 10–20
> • Pick a crayon colour and watch the number fill in with sparkles
> • Gentle feedback: going off the line never erases progress
>
> ⭐ REWARDS THAT TEACH
> • Earn up to 3 stars for every number
> • Confetti and a counting reward: trace 4 and count four strawberries out loud
>
> 🍎 TAP TO COUNT
> • Tap each object to hear it counted, then choose how many there are
>
> 🎈 POP THE NUMBER BALLOONS
> • Listen for a number and pop the matching balloons
>
> 👪 MADE FOR LITTLE LEARNERS
> • Every number is spoken aloud
> • Big buttons and simple screens for small hands
> • Works offline, so it's great for car rides and flights
> • Progress is saved on the device, with a reset option for parents
> • No accounts and no personal data collected
>
> Perfect for kids aged 2–5 learning numbers, counting and early handwriting.

## Categorisation

| Field | Suggested value |
| --- | --- |
| App or game | Game |
| Category | Educational |
| Tags | Numbers, Counting, Preschool, Kids, Handwriting |

A privacy policy URL is still required for apps that target children, even when no data is collected.

## Graphics

| Asset | File | Play requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | `store/icon-512.png` | 512 × 512 PNG, ≤ 1 MB (Play rounds the corners) |
| Feature graphic | `store/feature-graphic.jpg` | 1024 × 500 JPEG or 24-bit PNG |
| Phone screenshots | `store/phone/*.jpg` (7) | 2–8 images, 9:16, at least 1080 px for promotion |
| 7-inch tablet screenshots | `store/tablet-7/*.jpg` (4) | up to 8 images, 1200 × 1920 |
| 10-inch tablet screenshots | `store/tablet-10/*.jpg` (4) | up to 8 images, 1600 × 2560 |

The app's own icons (`icons/icon-*.png`, `icons/maskable-512.png`, `icons/icon.svg`) are generated by the same script.
